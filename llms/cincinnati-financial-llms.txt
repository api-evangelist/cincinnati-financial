## What the API program is

Cincinnati Insurance publishes an API Terms and Conditions Agreement that licenses "the Cincinnati
Insurance APIs" to a "Provider" — an integrator or vendor building an application against them.
The agreement is real, current and specific, but it is a legal instrument only. It names no
protocol, endpoint, authentication scheme, media type or industry standard. The technical terms
for each API live in per-API "API Policy(ies)" that the agreement incorporates by reference and
that Cincinnati does not publish.

- [API Terms & Conditions acceptance form](https://www.cinfin.com/legal/api-terms-conditions-acceptance): the entry point. Full name, email, title, organization, reCAPTCHA. On submit: "Your submission has been received. We'll be in contact with you shortly."
- [API Terms and Conditions Agreement (PDF)](https://edge.sitecorecloud.io/cincinna-x33xq9h6/media/Project/Cincinnati-Financial/CinFin/Files/api-terms-conditions.pdf): the licence itself.

Two clauses are worth knowing before you plan an integration:

- **No fee, for now.** Section 6: "Cincinnati Insurance does not currently charge a fee for accessing or using our APIs; however, Cincinnati Insurance may do so in the future."
- **No AI training on Cincinnati data without written consent.** Section 4(h) forbids a Provider from using Cincinnati Insurance Confidential Information "to train or uptrain any machine learning, neural network, deep learning (including large language models), predictive analytics, or other artificial intelligence" and requires prior written consent — including a description of the data, the tool, every processing location, and the purpose — before any AI tool touches it. Section 4(i) separately forbids deriving pricing indications, pricing models or rate analysis from claims data.

## What is not published

- No OpenAPI, Swagger, GraphQL SDL, AsyncAPI, WSDL or .proto at any anonymous URL.
- No developer portal, API reference, getting-started guide, changelog, status page, SDK, CLI or Postman collection.
- No OAuth or OpenID Connect discovery document on any cinfin.com host.
- No rate limits, quotas or plans. The agreement forbids exceeding limits it never states.
- No ACORD, AL3, CSIO, CIECA BMS, NGDS or other P&C domain-standard declaration.
- No `/llms.txt` of Cincinnati's own; `https://www.cinfin.com/llms.txt` returns 404.

## Security and disclosure

- [security.txt](https://www.cinfin.com/.well-known/security.txt): served at HTTP 200, one line — `Contact: mailto:bugbounty@cinfin.com`. No Expires, Policy or Encryption field, so it is below RFC 9116 minimums. Note that robots.txt also carries `Disallow: /.well-known/`.
- Domain posture (probed): TLS 1.3, HSTS with a one-year max-age and preload, DNSSEC signed, CAA restricted to DigiCert / Amazon / Amazon Trust / Let's Encrypt, SPF present, DMARC at `p=reject`.
